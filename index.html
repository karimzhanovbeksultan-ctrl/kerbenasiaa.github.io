Essential Packing Guide for Kyrgyzstan

Mountain weather changes rapidly — even in July, temperatures at high altitude drop near freezing at night

🧥 Warm & Layered Clothing

  • Warm fleece jacket or down puffer (essential for Son-Kul & evening yurt camps)
  • Waterproof & windproof shell jacket (rain and mountain wind protection)
  • Thermal base layer (top and bottom for high-altitude nights)
  • Breathable t-shirts and long-sleeve shirts for sunny daytime hikes

🥾 Footwear & Accessories

  • Broken-in waterproof hiking boots with strong ankle support and grip
  • Warm wool socks, beanie hat, and light gloves for mountain passes
  • Swimwear & quick-dry towel (for Issyk-Kul Lake & Altyn-Arashan hot springs)

🕶️ Sun & Health Protection

  • High-SPF sunscreen (SPF 50+) and UV-protection sunglasses (strong alpine sun)
  • Lip balm with SPF and personal first-aid kit
  • Reusable water bottle and power bank (limited electricity in remote yurt camps)
